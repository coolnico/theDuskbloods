## Who Is Senator Samir Patres in The Duskbloods?

**Senator Samir Patres** is a playable **Bloodsworn** in *The Duskbloods* — FromSoftware's 8-player PvPvE action RPG, exclusive to Nintendo Switch 2. Senate rapier, blood swamps, and dinosaur charges: a technical fighter who rewards clean defense and punishes people who think they are already winning.

He finished as a **B-tier technique / anti-carry** in our [Bloodsworn tier list](/guides/duskbloods-tier-list) — high skill, high reward. He is the pick when you want to **outbuild** the lobby and then **run over** the leader.

## Senator Samir Patres's Kit, Explained

Samir's kit is built around one idea: **defend cleanly, unlock early, then break the script**. Every tool either rewards defense, accelerates your build, or refuses to let them leave.

### Weapons: Senate Rapier & Soulbird Ring

| Slot | Weapon | What it does |
|------|--------|--------------|
| **Melee** | Senate Rapier | Structured dueling weapon. Your perfect-guard game lives here. |
| **Ranged** | Soulbird Ring | Mid-range pressure and poke while you set up the real plan. |

The rapier is the duel. The ring is how you stay dangerous while you build.

### Blood Arts: Bloodstake & Saurian Form

| Blood Art | Input idea | Best use |
|-----------|------------|----------|
| **Bloodstake** | Form a swamp of blood at the feet of foes. A colossal bloodstake rises from the swamp. | **Space lock.** Pin them in place; deny the circle-out. |
| **Saurian Form** | Assume the form of a carnivorous dinosaur, then charge and bite with powerful jaws. | **Script break.** Engage when they expect fencing, not a dinosaur. |

**Bloodstake** is the cage. **Saurian Form** is what you do when they try to walk around it.

### Aspects: Blood Disciple & Unshakable Sword

| Aspect | Effect | Why it matters |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| **Blood Disciple** | Lowers required **Bloodsworn skill level**. | Quiet economy. Your skills come online earlier than opponents expect. |
| **Unshakable Sword** | **Perfect guarding** boosts melee attack power. | Defense is damage. Train the guard window; stop mashing dodge. |

## Unshakable Sword & Blood Disciple: The Anti-Carry Loop

Samir is not a brawler. He is a **stat-check you did not see coming**.

### The guard loop (Unshakable Sword)

1. Perfect guard is your **damage buff**, not just survival.
2. Train the counter window. If you can parry, do not mash dodge.
3. After a clean guard, the rapier hits like a different character.

### The build edge (Blood Disciple)

- Required skill level is lower — **unlocks compound earlier**.
- Prioritize **stagger and blood economy** upgrades over flashy flair.
- By the time the lobby thinks you are "still building," you are already online.

### The rule of thumb

> If they think you will keep fencing, **they are already wrong**. Guard, unlock, then change the script.

## How to Play Senator Samir Patres: Phase-by-Phase Game Plan

The Duskbloods runs a four-phase Dusk Battle. Samir's job changes as the clock moves.

### Phase I — Build the edge

- Contest ritual sites with **structured** fights, not messy brawls.
- Use Soulbird Ring to poke; take the rapier duel when they overcommit.
- Spend the Blood Disciple advantage on **compounding** unlocks.

### Phases II–III — Punish the leaders

- The Grail culls low-Virtue Bloodsworn. **Stay on tempo** — your build is faster.
- Look for high-Virtue leaders. You are the anti-carry: perfect guard, then break them.
- **Bloodstake** when they try to collapse or rotate out of your duel.

### Phase IV — Run them over

- Final three often forgets the dinosaur. **Saurian Form** when they expect more fencing.
- Hold the rapier game until they disrespects your guard, then spend the buff.
- Make them play **your** duel, not the lobby's brawl.

## Senator Samir Patres Tips & Combos That Actually Work

### 1. Perfect guard is your damage buff

Train the guard counter window. Unshakable Sword turns defense into offense; do not mash dodge when you can parry.

### 2. Spend the skill-level edge

Blood Disciple lets you take Bloodsworn skills earlier. Prioritize unlocks that compound (stagger, blood economy) over raw flair.

### 3. Bloodstake then charge

Drop the swamp to pin them, then Saurian Form when they try to circle out of it.

### 4. Rapier for structure, ring for setup

Senate Rapier when the duel is honest. Soulbird Ring while you build and poke. Do not face-check with the charge.

### 5. Do not brawl the bruiser

Samir is difficulty 4 because the plan is technical. If you are trading 1v1 with Jan or Zork without guard value, you are playing the wrong game.

### 6. Change the script once per fight

They will adapt to the rapier. That is when the dinosaur arrives.

## Is Senator Samir Patres Good? Network-Test Verdict

**Senator Samir Patres is a B-tier high-skill, high-reward pick** in the August 2026 network test meta:

1. **Anti-carry.** He is built to break the player who is already winning.
2. **Build speed.** Blood Disciple is a quiet economy edge that compounds all match.
3. **Punishable floor.** Difficulty 4. Miss the guard window, waste the charge, or brawl without value — and you lose.

He is **not** a default recommendation. He is for players who already understand perfect guard timing, skill economy, and when to stop fencing.

## FAQ

### What is Senator Samir Patres's role in The Duskbloods?
**Senator Samir Patres is a technique / anti-carry duelist.** Unshakable Sword rewards perfect guards with melee damage, Blood Disciple unlocks skills earlier, Bloodstake locks space, and Saurian Form is the dinosaur charge that breaks the fencing script.

### How do you play Senator Samir Patres?
Fight structured duels and perfect guard for Unshakable Sword damage. Spend Blood Disciple's earlier unlocks on stagger and blood economy. Pin with Bloodstake, then Saurian Form when they try to leave. In the final three, change the script once.

### What do Unshakable Sword and Blood Disciple do?
**Unshakable Sword** boosts melee attack after a perfect guard. **Blood Disciple** lowers required Bloodsworn skill level, so your skills come online earlier.

### What weapons does Senator Samir Patres use?
Samir uses the **Senate Rapier** in melee and the **Soulbird Ring** at range. His Blood Arts are **Bloodstake** and **Saurian Form**.

### Is Senator Samir Patres good for beginners?
**No.** Difficulty is 4 / 5 and the plan is technical. Start on [Albert](/guides/duskbloods-albert-guide) until you can perfect guard on purpose.

### Senator Samir vs Zork — who should I pick?
Pick **Samir** if you want to break the leader with clean defense and a faster build. Pick **Zork** if you want to force fights and snowball in the open. If you face-check Samir, you die; if you face-check Zork, you brawl.
