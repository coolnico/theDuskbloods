## Can The Duskbloods Still Make 2026?

That is the question Switch 2 owners keep asking this month.

The Duskbloods was announced in April 2025 as a Switch 2 exclusive for **2026**. Since then we've had trailers, previews, character reveals, and a full closed network test in August. What we have never had is a **release date**.

It is early October. If the game still ships this year, you are looking at:

- **Less than three months** of calendar left
- A normal announce-to-launch runway compressed into that hole
- **GTA 6** landing in November and vacuuming up every oxygen molecule in the room

So searches like "is The Duskbloods delayed" and "The Duskbloods 2027" are spiking for a reason. Here's the clean version of where things stand.

## Why "Almost Certain Delay" Became the Consensus

On October 4, ComicBook ran a piece arguing the game is "all but guaranteed" to miss 2026. The argument is not complicated. That is why it landed.

### 1. The dignified reveal window is closing

A major release usually needs room for:

1. Locking a date
2. Opening pre-orders and retail orders
3. Preload and day-one patch
4. A few weeks of actual marketing

It is October. Even a surprise "it's December 3" drop tomorrow would feel rushed. Slip past mid-November and you are in shadow-drop territory — a terrible plan for an online PvPvE game that lives or dies on servers.

### 2. GTA 6 owns November

**GTA 6 ships in November 2026.** The audiences are not identical. They do not need to be. GTA 6 will still:

- Consume press and creator bandwidth
- Eat the "which game do I buy" decision
- Warp any publisher's marketing calendar around it

Dumping FromSoftware's big exclusive into the same month is how you turn a launch into a footnote. December or early 2027 is the grown-up dodge.

### 3. The network test's server problems are unfinished business

August's test was loud in both directions:

- **Good:** pace, PvPvE structure, Kin system, character kits — people came away impressed
- **Bad:** the first slot collapsed under server load, and PvP latency was rough

For an eight-player online game, **stability is the product**. The silence after the test looks a lot like "we're fixing the netcode before we talk dates," not "gold master is on the truck."

## Kitao: More Applications Than Nightreign

It was not all bad news. FromSoftware producer **Yasuhiro Kitao** said the network test drew **more applications than Elden Ring Nightreign's test**.

Context matters: Nightreign had a much larger install base. Duskbloods is sitting on Switch 2 alone. A smaller platform pulling a bigger queue means:

- The core audience is already here
- The problem is not demand — it is the calendar
- If this slips, it slips because of production and servers, not because nobody cares

Which is why the discourse stays hot even as the date stays cold. People want in. They just do not want to get burned.

## Is December 31 Still a Thing?

Search "The Duskbloods release date" and you will still hit **December 31, 2026**.

That number came from late-September database fills at IGN and IGDB. We broke it down in the [tracker piece](/latest/duskbloods-release-date-december-31-2026-trackers):

- **Not official.** Neither company has confirmed it.
- **December 31 is database shorthand** for "ships this year, day TBD."
- It is a holiday-window bet, not a leak.

Nothing in October has converted that placeholder into a real date. No trailer card. No eShop page. No preload clock. Treat December 31 as noise until Nintendo speaks.

The older threads do not help either. The [December 3 prediction](/latest/duskbloods-release-date-december-2026-prediction) was partly falsified by the Direct no-show. The [September 24 rumor](/latest/duskbloods-release-date-september24-denied) was denied. Stack those up and the only honest conclusion is: **there is no real release date on the internet right now.**

## Delay to 2027: How Likely?

Nobody credible is offering a percentage. What we can do is put the evidence side by side.

| Case for 2026 | Case for 2027 |

| Kadokawa keeps reconfirming 2026 | GTA 6 blocks November |
| Network test happened — this is not a concept project | Server / PvP issues may need another test |

Working read:

- **Confirmed delay:** no
- **Real delay risk:** high, and higher than it was in September
- **Most plausible scripts:** a compressed December drop, or a formal move into early 2027

Do not book time off. Do not pre-order a date a database invented. That advice has not changed since September.

## FAQ

### Is The Duskbloods delayed?

**Not officially.** The game is still labeled 2026. A move to 2027 is risk analysis, not an announcement.

### Will it still come out in 2026?

**Possible, not comfortable.** October is still dateless. A 2026 launch needs a very short reveal-to-release cycle to work.

### Is December 31, 2026 the release date?

**No.** That is a common placeholder in game databases. Nothing official backs it.

### If it slips, when would it land?

No reliable info. The crowd guess is **first half of 2027**. That is still a guess.

### Was the network test a success?

Mixed, with heat. Gameplay sentiment was strong and applications beat Nightreign. Servers and PvP latency were the weak spot — and the most likely reason nobody will print a date yet.
