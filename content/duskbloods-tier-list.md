## S Tier — Pick These First

<a id="albert"></a>
### Albert — S | The Default Answer

![Albert — Bloodsworn portrait](https://img.duskbloods.net/dusk/blood_relative/pc/charactor/albert.jpg?v=2)

**Kit**: Lorrik's Jeweled Saber · Submachine Gun · Blood Blade · Light of Hayern

Albert is the most approachable all-rounder in the network test. Clean melee stagger into SMG confirms covers almost every range, and **High Lorrik Blood** gives extra uses of blood-based skills — which means more uptime whenever you bank Virtue early.

- **Why S**: never a bad pick; strong in solos and in temporary alliances
- **Virtue game**: **Upriser** raises stagger against high-Virtue leaders — steal momentum instead of bullying weak players
- **Play it if**: you want one character to learn the whole game on

<a id="zork"></a>
### Zork — S | Be the Problem

![Zork — Bloodsworn portrait](https://img.duskbloods.net/dusk/blood_relative/pc/charactor/zork.jpg?v=2)

**Kit**: Undersea Cable · Speargun / Rockets · Missile · Human Rocket

Zork is a diving-suit wrecking ball. He grows max HP off kills (**Law of the Deep Sea**), disrupts sightlines, and forces everyone nearby to deal with him. Difficulty 2, but the floor is high: a Zork who survives culling is a final-three threat.

- **Why S**: tankiness + kill-to-grow snowballs through culling phases
- **Virtue game**: contest ritual sites early; stolen HP compounds with raw Virtue bonuses in the final phase
- **Play it if**: you like being the raid boss other teams must answer

## A Tier — Strong, With a Job

<a id="sniper-layla"></a>
### Sniper Layla — A | Own the Sightline

![Sniper Layla — Bloodsworn portrait](https://img.duskbloods.net/dusk/blood_relative/pc/charactor/layla.jpg?v=2)

**Kit**: Hieroglyph Katars · Verd Gatling Gun · Neb-ra Vanishing · Nighteye

Layla controls elevation and long sightlines, then vanishes to reset. On Lowanro City's multi-layered routes (streets, cave, forest, lake) she picks fights on her terms.

- **Why A**: elite when terrain favors her; weaker in forced close-range culling brawls
- **Play it if**: you already know the [map routes](/maps) and want free picks

<a id="bloodhand-jan"></a>
### Bloodhand Jan — A | Win Ugly, Win Long

![Bloodhand Jan — Bloodsworn portrait](https://img.duskbloods.net/dusk/blood_relative/pc/charactor/jan.jpg?v=2)

**Kit**: Bloodthirsty Axe · Blink · Surrender to My Blood · Bloodhand Lineage

Jan trades HP on purpose, counter-heals, and spellbinds minions to snowball. He is the best long-fight character in the test build.

- **Why A**: sustain beats burst in later phases; weaker if he gets chain-ambushed before healing online
- **Play it if**: you like attrition more than one-shot duels

## B Tier — Ceiling Is Real, Floor Is Painful

<a id="trang-lanh"></a>
### Trang Lanh — B | Decide the Fight From Stealth

![Trang Lanh — Bloodsworn portrait](https://img.duskbloods.net/dusk/blood_relative/pc/charactor/lanh.jpg?v=2)

**Kit**: Full Moon Blade · Darkmoon · Mama Nātha · Alluring Bride

Trang Lanh opens from stealth, drinks blood in crowds, and controls tempo. In the right hands she deletes a carry before Phase III. In the wrong hands she dies with zero Virtue to a culling.

- **Why B**: massive variance; punished hard by AI trespassers and random events
- **Play it if**: you already have match rhythm and want outplay tools

<a id="senator-samir-patres"></a>
### Senator Samir Patres — B | Outbuild, Then Run Them Over

![Senator Samir Patres — Bloodsworn portrait](https://img.duskbloods.net/dusk/blood_relative/pc/charactor/samir.jpg?v=2)

**Kit**: Senate Rapier · Bloodstake · Saurian Form · Unshakable Sword

Samir wants perfect guards, skill-level investment, and a Saurian Form finish. He is the anti-carry duelist — excellent into snowballing leaders, mediocre if you fall behind on Virtue.

- **Why B**: rewards lab time; loses too much tempo when parries miss
- **Play it if**: you like technical 1v1s and hate being stat-checked

## FAQ

### Is this the final The Duskbloods tier list?
**No.** It is a network-test preview. The full roster and launch balance are still unannounced.

### Who is the best Bloodsworn for beginners?
**Albert**, with **Zork** as a tankier alternative. Both are difficulty 2 and survive culling more reliably than stealth or tech picks.

### Do tier lists even matter in a PvPvE game?
They matter less than in a pure fighter. Virtue farming, Kin compacts, alliances, and trespassers can overturn a "worse" kit. Tiers here measure how often a character survives to Phase IV when players are of equal skill.
