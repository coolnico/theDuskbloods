## The Duskbloods Release Date: December 3 Prediction

Based on analysis from **IGN's Logan Plant** in his [September 6, 2026 predictions article](https://www.ign.com/articles/14-predictions-for-the-september-2026-nintendo-direct-and-zelda-40th-anniversary-direct), we believe **The Duskbloods will launch on December 3, 2026**. This prediction is based on several key factors:

### 1. Nintendo's Monthly Release Pattern
Nintendo typically releases one major first-party game per month. With **The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake** predicted for November 12, December remains the only available window for The Duskbloods in 2026.

### 2. Historical December Releases
When Nintendo publishes games in December, they usually launch early in the month:
- **Metroid Prime 4: Beyond** – December 4, 2025
- **Fire Emblem Engage** – December 8, 2023

December 3 falls perfectly into this early-December pattern.

### 3. Holiday Sales Strategy
Launching in early December maximizes holiday sales while avoiding direct competition with **Grand Theft Auto VI** (November 19) and other major releases.
