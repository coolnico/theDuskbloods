## What's New

### Match Structure
8 players compete across 3 rounds. After each round, the lowest-scoring players are culled. Only 3 make it to the final battle, where their health is boosted by accumulated **Virtue Points**.

### The Virtue System
Virtue is everything. You earn it by:
- Collecting **Flame Virtue** from map objects
- Killing players at **Ritual Sites** (Sword Virtue)
- Earning **Commendations** for performance
- Completing **Sigil Goals**

### Maps & Allies
The demo featured **Luenro City** — a map with 4 spawn quadrants, golden spires to summon allies, and teleport pads for instant fast travel. Bosses (Trespasses) can be tackled solo or with help from nearby players.

### Character Abilities
6 playable Bloodsworn in the demo (10+ at launch), each with unique weapons, abilities, and a roar mechanic that can stagger and convert enemies.

### Round 2 Events
Random world events like zeppelins, magnetic storms, and falling stars shake up the gameplay each round.

## Full Game Features
- 3 maps at launch
- Co-op boss battles
- Character customization
- Story progression between matches
- Alliance system (queue with friends or ally mid-match)
