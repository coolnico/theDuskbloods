> **TL;DR**: The September 24 release date floating around? Not confirmed. Nintendo has denied it. The only solid info we have: 2026, Switch 2 exclusive, exact date TBA.

---

## Where Did September 24 Come From?

A single preview article dropped the date **September 24, 2026** as *The Duskbloods*' launch day. It spread fast — a lot of players took it as gospel.

But here's the thing: **neither Nintendo nor FromSoftware ever officially confirmed that date.**

---

## Nintendo's Response

Two outlets quickly set the record straight:

- **Nintendo Wire** ran with: Nintendo confirms *The Duskbloods* is not releasing on September 24.
- **NintendoLife** followed up: Nintendo has denied the rumoured release date.

To be clear — **this isn't a delay. You can't delay something that was never officially dated.** It was an error in a preview piece that got picked up and repeated before anyone fact-checked it.

---

## What Do We Actually Know?

Here's where things stand right now:

- *The Duskbloods* is still targeting a **2026 release**
- It's a **Nintendo Switch 2 exclusive**
- The **exact release date has not been announced** — and September 24 has been specifically denied

Until Nintendo or FromSoftware make an official announcement, any specific date is just speculation.

---

## The Bottom Line

The "September 24" thing wasn't a leak, wasn't a delay — **it was inaccurate info from a preview that Nintendo has since corrected.**

As of now, the safe bet on *The Duskbloods*: **2026, Switch 2 only, date TBD.** Wait for the official word.
