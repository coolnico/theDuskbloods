## Who Is Zork in The Duskbloods?

**Zork** is a playable **Bloodsworn** in *The Duskbloods* — FromSoftware's 8-player PvPvE action RPG, exclusive to Nintendo Switch 2. He is **not** the 1980 Infocom text adventure of the same name. In-game, Zork is a diving-suit wrecking ball: a winged, deep-sea salvage figure who grows cannons and rocket packs out of his suit and steals maximum life from the players he kills.

He became the **meme favorite of the August 2026 network test** and finished as an **S-tier pick** in our [Bloodsworn tier list](/guides/duskbloods-tier-list). Hidetaka Miyazaki has spoken about the character with rare, near-maternal fondness in interviews ([IGN](https://sea.ign.com/the-duskbloods/247221/hidetaka-miyazaki-interview-fromsoftwares-boss-on-the-duskbloods-ttrpg-influences-the-nintendo-partn)) — a piece of Nintendo-flavored charm inside Twilight industrial body-horror.

## Zork's Kit, Explained

Zork's kit is built around one idea: **force the fight, win the fight, grow from the fight**. Every tool either starts an engagement or pays you for finishing one.

### Weapons: Undersea Cable & Speargun

| Slot | Weapon | What it does |
|------|--------|--------------|
| **Melee** | Undersea Cable | Honest, reliable close-range pressure. Good stagger into confirms. |
| **Ranged** | Speargun | Clean mid-range poke. Use it to soften targets before you commit. |

These are "honest tools" — they do not win games on their own. They set up the Blood Arts.

### Blood Arts: Missile & Human Rocket

| Blood Art | Input idea | Best use |
|-----------|------------|----------|
| **Missile** | Deploy the cannon from the diving suit's back, take a firing stance, then fire | **Punish stationary setups.** Catch people channeling, reviving, or holding a ritual. |
| **Human Rocket** | Deploy the back thrusters and charge forward in flight mode | **Force the engage.** Close distance, break formations, start the brawl Zork wants. |

**Human Rocket** is the signature move. It turns Zork into a guided threat that the whole lobby has to answer. **Missile** is the follow-up or the anti-camp tool.

### Aspects: Law of the Deep Sea & Dread Resonance

| Aspect | Effect | Why it matters |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| **Law of the Deep Sea** | Defeat a Bloodsworn → **steal their max HP**. If they defeat you later, **you lose it all**. | The snowball engine. Early kills compound through culling phases. |
| **Dread Resonance** | A non-human sonic howl. **Hold [L] + [ZR]** to keep it going. | Interrupt telegraphed attacks, inflate stagger, bully the final-phase duel. |

## Law of the Deep Sea: The HP Steal Mechanic

This is the reason to play Zork — and the reason people throw games on him.

**Law of the Deep Sea explained in one sentence**: when you defeat another Bloodsworn, you permanently gain max HP; if that same player later defeats you, you lose the HP you stole from them.

### How the snowball works

1. **Phase I** — farm Virtue and look for a clean Bloodsworn elimination (not just PvE).
2. **Each kill** raises your max HP ceiling. In a game where the Grail culls low-Virtue Bloodsworn in Phases II–III, extra HP is extra survival budget.
3. **Culling phases** — weaker players die to the Grail or to you. Hunt the wounded.
4. **Final three** — stolen max HP stacks with raw Virtue bonuses. You enter the last fight as the raid boss.

### How people throw

- **Dying to their victim.** If the player you robbed finishes you, the stolen HP is returned. Greed throws the lead.
- **Chasing kills into the open.** Zork wants controlled fights, not 1v3s in a field.
- **Ignoring Virtue.** HP does not stop the Grail. You still need Virtue to survive culling.

### The rule of thumb

> After a steal, **reset or play safer**. You are now worth more dead to the one person who can take it back.

## How to Play Zork: Phase-by-Phase Game Plan

The Duskbloods runs a four-phase Dusk Battle. Zork's job changes as the clock moves.

### Phase I — Establish the snowball

- Contest **ritual sites early**. Virtue now, kills later.
- Use Speargun to soften; commit with **Human Rocket** only when you have a real edge.
- Your first Bloodsworn elimination is the most important moment of the match.

### Phases II–III — Survive the Grail, hunt the weak

- The Grail culls low-Virtue Bloodsworn. **Do not be one of them.**
- Pick off players already damaged by culling or by other fights. Law of the Deep Sea wants **Bloodsworn eliminations**, not just farming.
- Save **Dread Resonance** to cancel telegraphed attacks when someone tries to collapse on you.

### Phase IV — Be the problem in the final three

- Stolen HP + Virtue bonuses make you the natural front-liner.
- Hold the howl in duels. Extended **Dread Resonance** inflates stagger and ruins combo timing.
- Force the other two to deal with you — then let them fight each other.

## Zork Tips & Combos That Actually Work

### 1. Kill to grow

Law of the Deep Sea wants **Bloodsworn eliminations**, not just PvE. Hunt players who are already weak from the Grail's culling.

### 2. Do not die to your victim

HP stolen is lost if the player you robbed finishes you. After a steal, reset or play safer — greed throws the lead.

### 3. Hold the howl

**Dread Resonance ([L] + [ZR])** extends while held. Use it to cancel telegraphed attacks or to bully a final-phase duel.

### 4. Human Rocket is an opener, not an escape

Do not burn it to run away if you can burn it to start the fight you want. Zork wins by choosing engagements.

### 5. Missile punishes greed

When someone channels, revives, or camps a ritual, **Missile** is the answer. Deploy, stance, fire.

### 6. Play the raid boss, not the assassin

Zork is difficulty 2 for a reason. You do not need fancy stealth. You need to **be the threat the lobby must address**, then cash in when they fail.

## Is Zork Good? Network-Test Verdict

**Yes — Zork is S-tier in the August 2026 network test meta**, for three reasons:

1. **Consistency.** Difficulty 2, high floor. Survives culling more reliably than stealth or tech picks.
2. **Snowball.** Law of the Deep Sea turns early leads into final-phase dominance.
3. **Forced attention.** Human Rocket makes him the player everyone has to answer — and that pressure wins games even when he does not top the kill board.

He is also the **community favorite** of the network test: the diving-suit winged figure with the stolen-HP rule set is pure FromSoftware cruelty wrapped in a weirdly lovable design.

## FAQ

### What is Zork's role in The Duskbloods?
**Zork is an aggressive tank / disruptor.** He forces fights with Human Rocket, punishes setups with Missile, and snowballs max HP through Law of the Deep Sea. He is the Bloodsworn other players must deal with.

### How do you play Zork?
Farm Virtue in Phase I, take clean Bloodsworn eliminations to grow max HP, survive Grail culling in Phases II–III, then enter the final three as the raid boss. Hold Dread Resonance to interrupt and bully duels. Never die to the player you robbed.

### What does Law of the Deep Sea do?
**Law of the Deep Sea steals max HP when you defeat another Bloodsworn.** If that player later defeats you, the stolen HP is returned. It is Zork's snowball engine — and his main throw condition.

### What weapons does Zork use?
Zork uses the **Undersea Cable** in melee and the **Speargun** at range. His Blood Arts are **Missile** and **Human Rocket**.

### Is Zork good for beginners?
**Yes.** Difficulty is 2 / 5, the floor is high, and the kit is straightforward: force fights, win fights, grow. New players who want a tanky pick over a technical one should start on Zork or [Albert](/characters/albert).

### Zork vs Albert — who should I pick?
Pick **Albert** if you want the safest all-rounder and a clean way to learn the game. Pick **Zork** if you want to snowball, force fights, and be the raid boss in the final three. Both are S-tier network-test picks.

### Is Zork the Infocom text adventure?
**No.** The Zork in The Duskbloods is a diving-suit Bloodsworn character. The 1980 *Zork* is a classic Infocom text adventure — different game, different era, same spelling.
