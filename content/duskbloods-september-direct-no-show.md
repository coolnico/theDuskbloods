> **TL;DR**: The Duskbloods skipped the September 9–10 Nintendo Direct entirely. Still labeled **2026**. No trailer. No date. Delay isn't official — but the window is closing fast.

---

## What Actually Happened at the September Direct

Nintendo packed roughly 45 minutes into its September 9 (or September 10, depending on your timezone) Direct. Switch 2 owners ate well:

- **Metroid Ravenous** — Samus is back in 2D
- **Kirby and the World Beyond** — first 3D Kirby, locked for 2027
- **Mario Kart World** — free update with classic tracks

For FromSoftware fans, though, the loudest moment was the silence. **The Duskbloods never showed up.**

---

## Why the Absence Hits Hard

A year of updates, trailers, hands-on previews, and a full closed network test in August — and the one thing players still don't have is a **release date**.

September felt like the obvious stage:

- The game still carries a **2026** window
- The network test is done, so the pre-launch runway should be open
- Holiday season is crowded; waiting longer only makes marketing harder

Instead, the show rolled from open to Kirby without a single frame of Zork.

---

## Delayed? Not Officially

Quick reality check: **nobody has announced a delay.**

Nintendo and FromSoftware still say **2026**. The window hasn't been pushed. This is not a delay — it's a calendar that refuses to fill in.

The problem is arithmetic:

- It is already **late September 2026**
- "Ship in 2026" now means announce date → open preorders/preload → launch in a very tight stretch
- The network test left open questions about server load and PvP latency. No word on whether those need more bake time

As IGN put it: *delayed? Not yet — but if they still want 2026, they're running out of time to make a proper announcement.*

---

## What This Means for the December 3 Prediction

We previously walked through why **December 3** fit Nintendo's release rhythm (see: [December 3 release date prediction](/latest/duskbloods-release-date-december-2026-prediction)).

After this Direct, that prediction splits into two tracks:

### Still plausible

- Nintendo drops the date in a **standalone Direct** or a press release
- A short announce-to-launch cycle (shadow-drop adjacent)
- Early December is still historically a Nintendo sweet spot

### Weaker than before

- Skipping September signals the game may not be **show-ready**
- If the plan really slipped to early 2027, staying quiet now is almost "cleaner"
- Hype is leaking into other fall releases while we wait

Bottom line: **the prediction is unproven, not disproven.** Any specific day remains a guess until Nintendo says otherwise.
