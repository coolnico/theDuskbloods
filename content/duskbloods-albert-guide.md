## Who Is Albert in The Duskbloods?

**Albert** is a playable **Bloodsworn** in *The Duskbloods* — FromSoftware's 8-player PvPvE action RPG, exclusive to Nintendo Switch 2. He carries the jeweled saber of House Lorrik and a modern submachine gun: aristocratic bloodline meeting industrial firepower in the Twilight of Humanity.

He finished as an **S-tier pick** in our [Bloodsworn tier list](/guides/duskbloods-tier-list) and is the character we recommend to anyone learning the game. Official materials describe his kit as a "walking argument" — clean melee pressure, mid-range confirms, and blood arts that scale with how well you manage Virtue.

## Albert's Kit, Explained

Albert's kit is built around one idea: **win neutral, break the leader, stay stocked**. Nothing is a gimmick — every tool is honest and answers a real fight problem.

### Weapons: Lorrik's Jeweled Saber & Submachine Gun

| Slot | Weapon | What it does |
|------|--------|--------------|
| **Melee** | Lorrik's Jeweled Saber | Clean spacing and stagger. The core of your neutral. |
| **Ranged** | Submachine Gun | Mid-range harass and kill confirms when they try to leave. |

These two cover the whole map. Saber for pressure, SMG for answers — you are never stuck in a bad range.

### Blood Arts: Blood Blade & Light of Hayern

| Blood Art | Input idea | Best use |
|-----------|------------|----------|
| **Blood Blade** | Fire a blade formed of your own blood. Scales with blood caliber. | **Reliable poke.** Chip, punish whiffs, force respect from range. |
| **Light of Hayern** | Raise the crystal of light on the casing and fire a beam. | **Burst confirm.** Use when they are staggered, pinned, or locked in an animation. |

**Blood Blade** is the everyday tool. **Light of Hayern** is the delete button once stagger is online.

### Aspects: High Lorrik Blood & Upriser

| Aspect | Effect | Why it matters |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| **High Lorrik Blood** | Blood-based Bloodsworn skills gain **additional uses**. | More Virtue and blood pickups = more Blades and beams per fight. |
| **Upriser** | Increases stagger potential against Bloodsworn with **high Virtue**. | Your anti-leader tool. Hit the person who is winning, not the one who is already dying. |

## High Lorrik Blood & Upriser: The Two Levers

Albert does not snowball like Zork. He **scales with resources** and **denies momentum**. Those are his two levers.

### Lever 1 — High Lorrik Blood (economy)

1. Every extra blood-art use is another poke or burst in the same fight.
2. Virtue and blood pickups feed this directly — farming is not just survival, it is ammo.
3. A stocked Albert can chain **Blood Blade → Light of Hayern** when someone is staggered.

### Lever 2 — Upriser (anti-leader)

- Grail culling rewards Virtue leaders. **Upriser makes those leaders your best targets.**
- Extra stagger against high-Virtue Bloodsworn means you can break setups and steal fights from the scoreboard.
- Do **not** waste it farming weak players. The whole point is to unmake the leader.

### The rule of thumb

> If someone is ahead on Virtue, **they are your assignment**. Saber to stagger, Upriser to break, SMG to confirm.

## How to Play Albert: Phase-by-Phase Game Plan

The Duskbloods runs a four-phase Dusk Battle. Albert's job changes as the clock moves.

### Phase I — Bank Virtue and blood

- Contest **ritual sites early**. Your kit gets stronger the more you stock.
- Use the SMG to harass without over-committing. Save Saber for real engagements.
- First blood-art upgrades matter more on Albert than almost anyone — High Lorrik Blood multiplies them.

### Phases II–III — Break the leader, survive the Grail

- The Grail culls low-Virtue Bloodsworn. **Stay stocked so you are not one of them.**
- Look at the board: whoever is winning is an **Upriser** target. Break them before the final three.
- Keep a Light of Hayern charge for emergencies — it is also a great punish when someone tries to collapse on you.

### Phase IV — Flex as the cleanest duelist

- You do not need gimmicks in the final three. Saber stagger, SMG confirm, beam when pinned.
- If you broke the leader earlier, the last fight is easier for everyone else **and** for you.
- Force honest 1v1s. Albert wins clean fights; he loses messy ones.

## Albert Tips & Combos That Actually Work

### 1. Bank Virtue early

High Lorrik Blood grants extra uses of blood-based skills — more Virtue and blood pickups mean more Blades and beams per fight.

### 2. Hunt high-Virtue targets

Upriser increases stagger against Bloodsworn who are already winning. Steal their momentum instead of farming weak players.

### 3. Saber for stagger, SMG for confirms

Open with melee to fill the stagger gauge, then swap to the SMG when they try to disengage.

### 4. Blood Blade is your neutral

Do not save it. Chip, whiff-punish, force respect. It scales with blood caliber, so early investment pays off all match.

### 5. Light of Hayern only when it lands

The beam is your burst. Fire it when stagger is locked, not as a random long-range coin flip.

### 6. Play the standard, not the highlight

Albert is difficulty 2 for a reason. You win by being the player who makes the fewest mistakes — not by looking flashy on the timeline.

## Is Albert Good? Network-Test Verdict

**Yes — Albert is S-tier in the August 2026 network test meta**, for three reasons:

1. **Consistency.** Difficulty 2, high floor, no dead ranges. Survives culling more reliably than specialists.
2. **Economy.** High Lorrik Blood turns farming into combat advantage — Virtue is literally ammo.
3. **Anti-leader.** Upriser means the person winning is also the person you can break. That is rare and it is powerful.

He is also the **best learning character** in the roster: one kit that teaches spacing, resource play, target priority, and phase tempo.

**Caveat**: this is a **network-test preview ranking**, not the final retail build. Launch balance is still unannounced. See our full [tier list](/guides/duskbloods-tier-list) for the rest of the roster.

## FAQ

### What is Albert's role in The Duskbloods?
**Albert is a balanced duelist.** He pressures with the jeweled saber, confirms with the submachine gun, and uses Blood Blade and Light of Hayern to poke and burst. High Lorrik Blood stocks extra skill uses; Upriser staggers high-Virtue leaders.

### How do you play Albert?
Bank Virtue and blood in Phase I so High Lorrik Blood stocks extra uses. In Phases II–III, use Upriser to break whoever is leading and survive Grail culling. In Phase IV, play clean: saber stagger, SMG confirm, beam when pinned.

### What do High Lorrik Blood and Upriser do?
**High Lorrik Blood** gives blood-based skills additional uses. **Upriser** increases stagger against Bloodsworn with high Virtue. Together they make Albert stronger the more he farms — and deadly against whoever is winning.

### What weapons does Albert use?
Albert uses **Lorrik's Jeweled Saber** in melee and a **Submachine Gun** at range. His Blood Arts are **Blood Blade** and **Light of Hayern**.

### Is Albert good for beginners?
**Yes — he is our default beginner pick.** Difficulty is 2 / 5, the kit is honest, and every mechanic he teaches (spacing, Virtue, target priority) transfers to the rest of the roster.

### Albert vs Zork — who should I pick?
Pick **Albert** if you want the safest way to learn the game and a kit that is never wrong. Pick **Zork** if you want to snowball, force fights, and be the raid boss in the final three. Both are S-tier network-test picks.
