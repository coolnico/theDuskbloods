> **TL;DR**: **Elden Ring** is the best pick if you want a huge single-player open world you can live in for months. **The Duskbloods** is the better fit if you want FromSoftware combat re-cut as **8-player PvPvE rounds** on Nintendo Switch 2 — shorter sessions, human rivals, and a launch that is still dateless as of October 2026. They share Miyazaki DNA, not the same loop.

---

## Quick Comparison

| | **The Duskbloods** | **Elden Ring** |
|---|---|---|
| **Core loop** | 8-player PvPvE Dusk Battle (4 phases) | Open-world solo exploration + bosses |
| **Multiplayer** | The point of the game | Optional co-op / invasions |

| **Match length** | Short rounds with Grail culling | Dozens of hours per playthrough |

| **Director** | Hidetaka Miyazaki | Hidetaka Miyazaki (with GRRM lore) |

If you only read one line: **Elden Ring is a place. The Duskbloods is a match.**

## 1. Structure: Open World vs Dusk Battle

**Elden Ring** drops you into the Lands Between with almost no rails. You can ignore story beats for 40 hours, farm a side dungeon, or walk into a late-game region and get deleted. Mastery is personal and mostly private.

**The Duskbloods** runs on **Dusk Battle** flow: up to 8 Bloodsworn enter the Lands of Dusk, farm Virtue, survive the Grail’s culling in Phases II–III, then the last three fight for the Blood Bestowal. There is a start, a clock, and other humans who want your spot. See the full phase breakdown on the [gameplay page](/gameplay).

**Who wins**: depends on whether you want *freedom* or *structure*.

---

## 2. Multiplayer: Optional vs Mandatory Core

In Elden Ring, multiplayer is a spice. You can finish the entire game offline and never touch it.

In The Duskbloods, PvPvE **is** the product. You fight AI trespassers and other Bloodsworn at once — the hybrid loop we unpacked in [What Is The Duskbloods PvPvE?](/latest/duskbloods-pvpve-explained). FromSoftware also confirmed skill-based matchmaking so new players meet new players ([matchmaking notes](/latest/duskbloods-beginner-matchmaking)).

There *is* a single-player mode, but anyone comparing “is it like Elden Ring?” usually means the multiplayer fight — and that fight has no Elden Ring equivalent.

## 3. Character & Build Identity

| | The Duskbloods | Elden Ring |
|---|---|---|
| **Identity** | Fixed Bloodsworn (12+ promised; 6 in [network test roster](/characters)) | Build-your-own Tarnished |
| **Weapons** | Signature kit per character (saber+SMG, axe, katana, etc.) | Hundreds of weapons, Ashes of War |
| **Specials** | Blood Arts + Aspects | Sorceries, Incantations, Skills |
| **Wild card** | **Kin** compacts (Ring / Blade / Eye circles) | Spirit Ashes, Great Runes, summons |

Elden Ring wins on **sandbox freedom**. The Duskbloods wins on **readable kits and counterplay** — if you see Albert’s jeweled saber, you already know the plan. Full kits: [Bloodsworn page](/characters) · [Kin system](/kin).

---

## 4. Difficulty & “Fairness”

Elden Ring difficulty is mostly **PvE knowledge**: learn the boss, bring the right tool, or out-level it. Cheese is a feature.

The Duskbloods difficulty is **PvPvE variance**: a clean duel can turn into a third-party ambush, a trespasser wave, or a culling because someone else farmed better. Winning is partly skill, partly Virtue tempo, partly not being the player everyone decides to delete.

If losing to *other people* tilts you harder than losing to Malenia, stay with Elden Ring.

---

## 5. Time & Platform Reality

- **Elden Ring**: available now, every current platform, 80–150 hour playthroughs, huge mod/creator scene.
- **The Duskbloods**: **Switch 2 exclusive**, still officially 2026 with **no release day** (see the [release-date tracker](/release-date)), built for shorter sessions and online population health.

You cannot “just buy The Duskbloods this weekend.” You *can* buy Elden Ring tonight. That alone settles many comparisons.

## Who Should Play What

### Choose **Elden Ring** if you want
- A massive solo open world and a story you can soak in
- Offline play, mods, and endless build experimentation
- Combat you can learn at your own pace

### Choose **The Duskbloods** if you want
- FromSoftware combat tuned for **8-player PvPvE**
- Shorter, sharper matches with human reads and alliances
- Switch 2 exclusives and the next Miyazaki multiplayer swing

### Play **both** if
You like Souls combat and also like Hunt / Tarkov-style tension. They do not really compete for the same hour of your week.

## FAQ

### Is The Duskbloods basically Elden Ring 2?
**No.** Shared studio and director DNA, different format. Elden Ring is a solo open-world RPG; The Duskbloods is a match-based PvPvE action game.

### Is The Duskbloods like Elden Ring Nightreign?
Closer in spirit (multiplayer-focused FromSoftware experiment), but Duskbloods is its own PvPvE game on Switch 2 — not a Nightreign expansion.

### Does The Duskbloods have the same build depth as Elden Ring?
Unlikely at launch. Expect fewer weapons, but denser per-character kits, Kin compacts, and blood history choices inside a match.

### Can I play The Duskbloods on PC?
No. It is a **Nintendo Switch 2 exclusive** (as of October 2026).

### Which is better for beginners?
If you are new to FromSoftware: **Elden Ring** teaches at your pace. If you are new but want multiplayer: **The Duskbloods** has SBMM to protect first-timers — see [beginner matchmaking](/latest/duskbloods-beginner-matchmaking).
