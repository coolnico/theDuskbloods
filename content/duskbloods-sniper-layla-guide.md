## Who Is Sniper Layla in The Duskbloods?

**Sniper Layla** is a playable **Bloodsworn** in *The Duskbloods* — FromSoftware's 8-player PvPvE action RPG, exclusive to Nintendo Switch 2. She is a scholar-hunter with Hieroglyph Katars and a sniper rifle: archive and battlefield in one kit, with a bloodline that turns her into a swarm of insects when cornered.

She finished as an **A-tier specialist** in our [Bloodsworn tier list](/guides/duskbloods-tier-list) — the long-range pick. If you want to decide who dies before they know you are there, Layla is how.

## Sniper Layla's Kit, Explained

Layla's kit is built around one idea: **own the sightline, then leave when they come for you**. Every tool either extends your range, punishes rotations, or gets you out.

### Weapons: Hieroglyph Katars & Sniper Rifle

| Slot | Weapon | What it does |
|------|--------|--------------|
| **Melee** | Hieroglyph Katars | Last resort when they force the pocket. |
| **Ranged** | Sniper Rifle | Your main weapon. Punish anyone rotating carelessly. |

The rifle is the character. The katars are how you survive when the plan fails.

### Blood Arts: Neb-ra Vanishing & Verd Gatling Gun

| Blood Art | Input idea | Best use |
|-----------|------------|----------|
| **Neb-ra Vanishing** | Assume the form of a swarm of insects and move to a distant location. The afterimage confuses foes. | **Escape and reposition.** Bail out, bait shots with the afterimage, re-peek from a new angle. |
| **Verd Gatling Gun** | Extract a giant Gatling gun from your body, prepare, then unleash a rapid-fire barrage. | **Panic button or finisher.** Close-range save, or a brutal end if you set it up from high ground. |

**Neb-ra Vanishing** is how you reset. **Verd Gatling Gun** is what you use when they finally reach you.

### Aspects: Neb-ra Lineage & Nighteye

| Aspect | Effect | Why it matters |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| **Neb-ra Lineage** | HP gradually restores toward **1/2 of max**. Goldblood Stakes restore **less** than for others. | Passive sustain. Do not plan around burst heals — Stake economy is worse for you. |
| **Nighteye** | Longer range for **lock-ons and observation**. | The whole reason to pick Layla. See further, lock further, shoot first. |

## Nighteye & the Long Angle

Layla is not a duelist. She is a **sightline editor**.

### The setup

1. Play **elevation**: clock towers, roofs, long streets. Tight corridors are someone else's game.
2. Nighteye lets you **observe and lock** beyond normal range. Information is damage.
3. The Sniper Rifle punishes anyone rotating carelessly between landmarks.

### The reset (Neb-ra Vanishing)

- When the fight collapses, **vanish to reset, not to style**.
- Use the afterimage to bait shots, then re-peek from a new angle.
- Do not waste it as a flashy engage. It is your life.

### The rule of thumb

> If they can see your silhouette, you are in the wrong place. **Shoot first, move before they answer.**

## How to Play Sniper Layla: Phase-by-Phase Game Plan

The Duskbloods runs a four-phase Dusk Battle. Layla's job changes as the clock moves.

### Phase I — Establish the angle

- Contest ritual sites **from range**. Nighteye observation is free Virtue info.
- Do not face-check. Let others spend cooldowns; you take the shot.
- First rotation you punish can decide the early lobby.

### Phases II–III — Pick the weak, survive the cull

- The Grail culls low-Virtue Bloodsworn. **Stay stocked** and watch the board.
- Hunt people rotating between landmarks. Do not chase into buildings.
- Save **Neb-ra Vanishing** for third parties and culling collapses.

### Phase IV — Control the final sightline

- Final three is often open ground. Own the long line before they close.
- If they push you: vanish, reposition, or **Verd Gatling Gun** when they commit.
- Do not duel bruisers in a phone booth. Make them walk into your rifle.

## Sniper Layla Tips & Combos That Actually Work

### 1. Play elevation, not honor

Clock towers, roofs, and long streets are your kit. If you are in a tight corridor, you are playing someone else's game.

### 2. Vanish to reset, not to style

Neb-ra Vanishing is an escape and reposition tool. Use the afterimage to bait shots, then re-peek from a new angle.

### 3. Respect the Stake economy

Neb-ra Lineage regenerates HP toward half, but Goldblood Stakes heal you less. Do not plan around burst heals.

### 4. Observation is damage

Nighteye lock-ons give you information before the shot. Call rotations; shoot the ones who ignore them.

### 5. Gatling is a finisher, not a sniper tool

Verd Gatling Gun is close-range panic or a set-piece finish. Do not use it as a long-range coin flip.

### 6. Do not hold the same angle twice

After one kill, **move**. Good players will not walk into the same sightline.

## Is Sniper Layla Good? Network-Test Verdict

**Yes — Sniper Layla is A-tier in the August 2026 network test meta**, for three reasons:

1. **Range.** Nighteye is a structural advantage in an 8-player lobby full of landmarks.
2. **Pick power.** The first clean rotation kill often decides the phase.
3. **Escape.** Neb-ra Vanishing means a failed angle is not a dead angle.

She is a **specialist**, not a default pick. Difficulty 3 and a plan that requires discipline: do not chase, do not hold the same angle, do not heal through burst.

## FAQ

### What is Sniper Layla's role in The Duskbloods?
**Sniper Layla is a long-range pick / scout.** Nighteye extends lock-on and observation, the Sniper Rifle punishes rotations, and Neb-ra Vanishing gets her out when the fight collapses. Verd Gatling Gun is the close-range panic button.

### How do you play Sniper Layla?
Play elevation and long streets. Use Nighteye to observe and lock, then shoot people rotating between landmarks. Vanish to reset when they push. Save Verd Gatling Gun for close-range panic or a set-piece finish.

### What do Nighteye and Neb-ra Lineage do?
**Nighteye** extends lock-on and observation range. **Neb-ra Lineage** gradually restores HP toward half of max, but Goldblood Stakes heal you less than other Bloodsworn.

### What weapons does Sniper Layla use?
Layla uses the **Sniper Rifle** at range and **Hieroglyph Katars** in melee. Her Blood Arts are **Neb-ra Vanishing** and **Verd Gatling Gun**.

### Is Sniper Layla good for beginners?
**Only if you already understand rotations and culling.** Difficulty is 3 / 5 and the plan is unforgiving if you face-check. New players should start on [Albert](/guides/duskbloods-albert-guide).

### Sniper Layla vs Zork — who should I pick?
Pick **Layla** if you want to own sightlines and pick fights from range. Pick **Zork** if you want to force fights and snowball in the open. Zork closes the gap — vanish before he does.
